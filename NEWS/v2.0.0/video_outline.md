# EHR Browser v2.0.0 tour — video outline

Window 1280 × 800. Each title card is shown ~2 s before its steps. Titles and subtitles follow `whats_new.md`.

1. **Search for any medical code**
   *Diagnoses, procedures, drugs, measurements, standard and non-standard*
   - Search "Asthma", click **Asthma SNOMED**
   - Switch to **Hierarchy**

2. **Person Counts**
   *How many people have each code in the concept's tree, and how many share several of them*
   - Click **Person Counts**

3. **Prevalence**
   *Share of the population with each concept, year by year*
   - Click **Prevalence**

4. **Incidence**
   *Share of the population with a new case of each concept, year by year*
   - Click **Incidence**

5. **New filters**
   *Limit the plots to a time range, age or visit type, and use Level and Class to simplify large trees*
   - Click **Record Counts**
   - Set **Level** to 2
   - In **Age**, click 0-9, 10-19, 20-29 and 30-39 to leave them out
   - Drag the **Time range** start to 2011 (last: changing another filter afterwards resets the time range)

6. **Lab Values**
   *Distribution of measured values for lab tests*
   - Search "C reactive protein", click **C reactive protein|Mass Concentration|Moment in time|Blood, Serum or Plasma**
   - Click **Lab Values**
